UniBites: Making Campus Eating Easier

What is our project? A Cafeteria Food Ordering System

Our project is a web-based cafeteria food ordering system. It allows students to browse food, add items to a cart and place orders.

Project Features

  • Login and registration
  • Browse menu
  • Shopping cart
  • Place orders
  • Payment
